# Build 133 tide provider-use containment

On 16 October 2026 Shane confirmed that no written WorldTides permission for navigation/safety-critical use was known. The current [WorldTides terms](https://www.worldtides.info/terms), updated 9 October, retain an explicit navigation/safety restriction alongside their informational-use and caching provisions. Paid access, billing approval, an acknowledgement sheet and a matching LAT datum are not evidence of provider permission for clearance calculations.

`services/tides/navigationUse.ts` is a fixed closed source boundary. It has no preference, account entitlement or environment override. `fetchTideCurve` returns `null` before cache, chart access or upstream requests. Neither positive nor negative WorldTides predictions reach route feasibility, departure windows, chart tide offsets, route colouring or chart-ahead alarms. The Pi vault applies the same mirrored boundary to optional IPC tide payloads; a phone-provided LAT label cannot confer permission.

Null means unavailable tidal clearance, not a measured zero-height tide. Shallow-gate departure sweeps retain `unknown`, null UKC and no best departure; tracer windows remain unavailable and shallow stretches receive no tide credit. Chart screening continues against charted depths. Its route report says tidal clearance is unverified, and the depth control says `CHART DATUM — tidal clearance unverified`. Chart coverage is a separate geometric coverage result and does not prove the actual water level. Negative surge below datum is not modelled by assuming chart datum.

Previously saved tide-window verification cannot authorize Cast Off or produce a green checked Follow status. Fresh cached labels cannot bank shallow legs as verified. Saved dry-tail readers retain charted depth/draft facts but remove unproven tide tops and floating windows before producing current advice. Chart-only saved verification remains usable under its existing geometry, draft, chart and age checks.

Glass high/low predictions, informational graphs, hourly interpolation, source selection and attribution remain on the existing display API. Official display tables do not become navigation sources through this change. Exact chart-datum proof and the existing privacy buckets remain intact behind the closed boundary; a future approved source must pass a separately reviewed provider-use and datum contract. Pure synthetic interpolation and clearance maths remain tested without enabling WorldTides runtime credit.

Regression coverage exercises the actual closed service with an otherwise LAT-matched ten-metre prediction that would incorrectly clear a one-metre charted shallow. The alarm remains, no navigation WorldTides request occurs, live-depth curves and route ceilings remain absent, shallow-gate UKC stays unknown, and Glass still receives labelled predictions. Separate tests cover tracer non-disclosure, pure datum refusal, chart controls and Pi IPC attempts to provide positive or negative tide credit. This containment makes no legal conclusion about a future written agreement or a different provider.
