## TestFlight metadata

### Beta App Description

Thalassa is an offline-first marine weather, passage-planning, vessel-management, and watchkeeping companion built for sailors. It brings marine conditions, passage readiness, float plans, voyage and diary logging, vessel stores and maintenance, NMEA instruments, chart management, Anchor Watch, Guardian, pinned native Pi/Boat Network integration, and an Apple Watch companion into one cockpit-focused app. Community precise-track sharing remains unavailable in this public-beta candidate.

This public beta is free. Thalassa is an advisory aid, not certified navigation, distress, or safety equipment. Skippers must use official current charts and publications, maintain a proper watch, verify forecasts and instrument data independently, carry suitable independent alarms and communications, and exercise normal seamanship.

### Beta App Review Notes

- Email one-time code is the only enabled authentication path. A reviewer may use an email address they control; no paid entitlement is required.
- Native Sign in with Apple source is present but compile-time default-off. The Apple button must remain hidden in this candidate. It must not be enabled until the complete TN3194 token lifecycle, Apple credentials, server-notification processor, revocation, and account-deletion behaviour are deployed and verified.
- The first-run maritime disclaimer is intentionally mandatory. Scroll through and accept it before continuing.
- Location is requested at point of use. Anchor Watch requires verified Always location access, usable notifications including the signed Time Sensitive path, and a freshly confirmed audible sound check before it can be treated as testable.
- Anchor Watch uses ordinary application audio and user-controllable Time Sensitive notifications. It has no Critical Alerts entitlement and cannot promise forced volume or an unconditional Silent-mode or Focus bypass.
- Raspberry Pi / Boat Network integration is available only in a native build containing the Pi TLS pinning verifier. Pairing pins the Pi public key and native requests use HTTPS; web builds, old native shells, and stripped builds fail closed without a plaintext or unverified fallback. Discovery, pairing, provisioning, diary relay, chart sync, and Pi-hosted Signal K/AVNav/N2K still require paired-device smoke. The ENC Library accepts structurally validated, already-converted packs from Files, direct HTTPS, or the paired Pi; because publisher authenticity cannot be verified, they remain visibly unverified reference overlays and are ignored by hazards, route verification, Route Tracer, and Cast Off. The beta does not decode raw or encrypted charts.
- Gmail access is paused. The beta does not use Gmail tokens or inbox content.
- Apple Music is fail-closed in the client and the local `musickit-token` function source. Its route, global player, and Calypso tools are unavailable, this candidate requests no MusicKit entitlement, and the server hold must be deployed before release.
- Calypso proactive vessel alerts are compile-time fail-closed. The beta does not monitor battery, depth, GPS, engine, or NMEA state after backgrounding or termination; use dedicated alarms and normal watchkeeping.
- AISHub contribution is disabled. Native NMEA/AIS reception may still be tested, but the beta cannot transmit NMEA sentences to AISHub.
- Billing and subscriptions are disabled. Public-beta access is free; Upgrade and Restore must not imply that a purchase is available.
- No unverified commercial ENC/S-57/S-63 package is distributed. The production bundle contains no local demo ENC. Testers may import only already-converted packs they are legally entitled to use; imported packs are shown as unverified references and cannot satisfy any safety gate. Raw S-57/S-63 and o-charts remain in their approved chart system; the beta does not decrypt them.
- Private recipe photos are unavailable until a private bucket and signed-URL flow ship. Community Galley photos are public content and must be presented as such before upload.
- Guardian proximity and bounded alert broadcasting are enabled in the candidate UI. The linked migration ledger records `20260804191000_guardian_presence_privacy.sql` and `20260804192000_guardian_broadcast_contract.sql` remotely, enforcing explicit arming, caller-owned recent presence, bounded discovery, and quotas in reviewed source. Ledger presence does not prove live behavior: an authenticated arm/disarm/discovery/broadcast smoke remains mandatory. The AIS ingest worker watchdog is a separate exact `GUARDIAN_WATCHDOG_ENABLED=true` opt-in that defaults off; that worker revision still must be deployed and verified off unless deliberately enabled.
- Community precise-track browsing, import, and sharing are held. Local voyage recording remains available, but the owner-only database policies remain undeployed and Public Voyage Log publishing is a separate opt-in surface that stays off by default.
- The complete account-deletion UI and client cleanup implementation remain in source, and approved local source now also contains a durable tombstone, write fences, exact owner-linked Storage inventory/verification, survivor/direct-identifier scrubs, and an auth-last resumable Edge workflow. The committed production profile still keeps `VITE_ACCOUNT_DELETION_ENABLED=false`: Settings shows an honest temporary-unavailable boundary with `privacy@thalassawx.com`, and the service rejects before the destructive Edge invocation. The local implementation is not deployment evidence. Account creation remains blocked until the exact migration and Function are independently reviewed, deployed, and pass authenticated deletion, interruption/resume, concurrent-write, Storage-race, and re-authentication smoke tests.
- Apple requires apps that support account creation to offer deletion in-app; the email contact is not the release solution for Thalassa. See [Offering account deletion in your app](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/).
- The canonical profile parks every CMEMS/MPA overlay. Hosted verification returned missing dual-slot assets and failing virtual manifests for currents, sea-surface temperature, chlorophyll, and MPA, so all seven marine-data switches remain false and unreachable. They may be re-enabled only after immutable generation assets, inactive-slot publication with two valid v2 discovery slots, bounded client reads, SHA-256 and exact schema checks, actual source-time freshness/coverage, finite/plausible values, mixed-generation rejection, attribution, and hosted end-to-end smokes pass.
- Marine generation assets use ISO-week shards. Publishers seed `manifest-v2-a.json` and `manifest-v2-b.json`, then replace only the inactive slot; clients request the proxy-selected virtual `manifest-v2.json`. Cutover is mandatory and two-phase: Phase A keeps the Vercel production alias on the previous deployment while the four public-beta publishers are run manually and both v2 slots are verified for every feed. Only Phase B deploys the new proxy and clients. After deployment and CDN purge, verify virtual-manifest selection plus bare and cache-busted `manifest.json`, `mpa.geojson`, and stable `hNN.bin` requests returning `410`. The CMEMS v1 bridge is emergency-only, exact opt-in, default-off, and irreversibly expires at `2026-08-20T00:00:00Z`; it is not a Phase A dependency. The map fetches one selected CMEMS frame, owns no more than two decoded frames / 32 MiB and one CMEMS marine product, and releases stale work on scrub, hide, refresh, or failure. Waves run every 12 hours with one exact three-hour source-age margin (15 hours total); currents remain at 12 hours. The routing adapter performs no CMEMS network request and supplies no current field in this beta.
- MPA popup classes are explicitly inferred from CAPAD metadata, require verification of current fishing and anchoring rules with the managing authority, and are labelled as neither legal advice nor navigation. The class must never be presented as definitive permission or prohibition.
- The LINZ/Maritime NZ warning writer is lockfile-installed, audited, tested, sandboxed, secret-isolated, non-cancelling, and fail-closed in local source. It is not a live release boundary until the exact master-branch workflow passes against Cloudflare, scoped GitHub secrets, the intended table, and the served feed. Its current upsert/delete reconciliation is not atomic and must be explicitly accepted or replaced by a reviewed least-privilege transactional RPC.
- The app declares `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`; it ships no custom or non-exempt cryptography.
