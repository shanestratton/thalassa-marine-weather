# Worldwide facilities (140-01)

Obs → Layers → Sea → Facilities is an independent, default-off reference layer for explicitly mapped OSM marinas, fuel docks, boat ramps and suction pump-outs. It lazy-loads only on the foreground Obs surface. Leaving Obs, turning it off, changing the account, replacing the map style or deleting the optional public cache retires its map data, pending reads and popups. Offline and Satellite Mode use already saved catalogue data without WAN requests.

The separately published `facilities-world` namespace requires all eight reviewed continental cohorts. It does not change the existing regional build-138 `facilities` contract, anchorage/mooring datasets, quotas or routing inputs. The client validates the original OSM identities, source clocks and cohort hashes. Current geographic acquisition and publication are **prepared source only**; no new global extract, live R2 generation, timer, server credential or hardware change has been made. An unpublished or missing catalogue is shown as unavailable, never as worldwide coverage.

The existing public catalogue reader retains its limits: at most 16 two-degree root cells, 16 selected leaves, 10,000 returned records, two concurrent requests, 15-second request timeout and 2 MiB JSON responses. The facilities display additionally selects the nearest 2,000 records within 50 NM of its loaded centre, requires zoom 7 or closer, and reloads after 10 NM of movement. Truncation or missing leaves remain partial. Nearby independent OSM objects are not fuzzy-merged.

Popups show original source-object links, representative-area-centre labels when applicable, original source age and **Community data, may be incomplete**. Access, permission, opening hours, service availability and approach depth remain unverified. The display does not create routes or feed watches, collision alarms or clearance calculations. Popups use safe DOM text, bounded internal scrolling, a marker-bound corner anchor and 44 px controls; they close on pan or resize. The existing single Credits control contains the ODbL credit and saved, unavailable, partial and older-source details. Its outer dimensions are unchanged.

The pipeline and concrete resource/deployment envelope are documented in [world-facilities-deployment-plan.md](./world-facilities-deployment-plan.md). Original tag distinctions and rights are based on [OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [Geofabrik extract documentation](https://download.geofabrik.de/technical.html), [slipways](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:leisure=slipway), [marine fuel](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:waterway=fuel) and [holding-tank disposal tags](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Toilet_Holding_Tank_Disposal). Land/RV chemical-disposal basins and ordinary road filling stations do not become marine pump-outs or fuel docks.
