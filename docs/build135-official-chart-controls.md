#135-06 official chart presentation controls

The top-centre map menu offers **Charts & detail** on Obs and the planner. It opens a lazy sheet with paper sources intersecting the current camera, opacity, Base/Standard/All detail presets, and the separate free NOAA ENC catalogue. Selecting a paper source never flies the camera. The catalogue owns original ZIP retention, weekly revision checks and download/import; this control does not cache a raster area or read purchased chart files.

The opt-in **ENC only** base turns on the existing ENC stack, uses its opaque depth/land treatment, and hides relief, imagery and known basemap geometry. A neutral grey backdrop remains outside chart coverage, with the existing no-coverage notice. Routes, AIS, hazards and chart sources remain untouched. Leaving restores prior basemap visibility. Paper overlays stand down in this base; explicitly selecting a paper chart returns to a plain relief/Light background. Obs keeps the account base; the planner keeps its device-local base.

Category presets map All→scrubber0, Standard→3 and Base→6. They share the existing scrubber's protected depth bands, safety contour, hazards and isolated-danger pointers. They are presentation presets, not a claim that Thalassa is a certified S-52/ECDIS system. A manual slider change clears the explicit category and restores the existing browse/default rule.

## Sources verified16 October2026

- [NOAA's official service list](https://www.nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/data/gis-data-and-services.html) links traditional chart symbology to `MCS/NOAAChartDisplay` and electronic S-52 symbology to `MCS/ENCOnline`. The inherited paper option incorrectly requested ENCOnline with a depth-unit parameter; this is corrected. NOAA says the derived images update weekly. These images are not an installed-cell edition check or carriage-compliant official ENC redistribution.
- Fixed public Norfolk tile z10/x294/y398 returned200 image/png from both endpoints: traditional21,816bytes, electronic25,690bytes, both256×256. Neither response supplied ACAO. Public proof images are outside the source tree. No boat location or purchased chart was requested.
- The Node `api/noaa-chart.ts` proxy accepts only style=paper|ecdis and canonical z3–18/x/y coordinates. It derives Mercator bounds itself and pins the two official service paths. It refuses extra/duplicate fields, redirects, non-PNG content, wrong dimensions, responses above1MiB and requests exceeding12seconds. It returns public browser/native CORS and caches successes1hour. Source failures return502/no-store. Vite dev/preview call the same handler; the production proxy must be deployed before production image availability is claimed.
- [LINZ's official tile specification](https://www.linz.govt.nz/guidance/data-service/linz-data-service-guide/map-tile-services/lds-web-map-tile-service-specifications) covers hydrographic chart images, API-key access and CC BY4.0. The existing public key/tileset4758 is retained; account overrides remain fenced and URL-encoded. The control credits Land Information New Zealand and links to its source. This change does not enable LINZ ENC ingestion.
- NOAA images remain withheld until the current account accepts the exact versioned [NOAA agreement](https://charts.noaa.gov/ENCs/ENC_Agreement.shtml). The hook enforces this even for a previously selected source, clears Mapbox sources at an account switch, and ignores stale consent completion. The sheet displays the official origin and the derived/not-certified wording.

Camera region checks are coarse, local and date-line aware, not proof that every pixel is charted. The public service sees tile coordinates, never a literal boat fix. A raster selection cannot turn off fallback sea marks merely because an image was requested. Paper images are removed in Satellite Mode; downloaded free ENC cells remain the offline path. Opening the menu/sheet does not query an imagery provider. Weekly catalogue metadata checks are separately gated by the catalogue service's account/consent/network/cadence policy.
