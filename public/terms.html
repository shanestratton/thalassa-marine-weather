2. Description of Service

Thalassa is a marine weather and vessel management application that provides:

  • Marine weather forecasts and environmental data

Crew List Phone Verification

Before a Find a Crew or Find a Skipper profile can be published, Thalassa asks you to verify control of a phone number. The number is sent to Twilio Verify solely to deliver and check one-time verification codes. It is not displayed on your public profile, disclosed to other members, used by Thalassa for marketing, or shared with third parties for marketing.

Thalassa does not retain the full phone number or one-time code in its database. During a verification attempt, the only retained representations of the phone number are the last four digits and a keyed one-way fingerprint used to recognise the same number and deter duplicate or abusive profiles. Thalassa also temporarily retains the attempt status and times and a Twilio verification reference for security and delivery auditing and to complete the attempt; the reference is not a phone number. Attempt records are automatically deleted within 25 hours. After successful verification, those same limited fields form your current verification record and remain until you remove verification or delete your account. The fingerprint is not dialable and cannot be used to recover the number without Thalassa's private server-side key. Twilio separately processes and retains the number and related delivery data for limited periods as Thalassa's verification provider under Twilio's privacy terms.

You may ask Thalassa to remove the retained phone-verification record at any time by emailing privacy@thalassawx.com. Removal immediately deletes Thalassa's stored last four digits, verification-record fingerprint, verification status and time, and provider reference. To limit repeated SMS abuse, Thalassa also keeps separate opaque keyed quota tokens derived from the number and the request's network address, together with a quota window and count; the raw network address is not stored in those records. These non-dialable tokens may remain for up to 25 hours after verification removal solely to stop repeated SMS abuse. They cannot be used to contact you or recover the number or network address without Thalassa's private server-side key and are then deleted. A Crew List profile cannot remain published after removal unless a phone number is verified again.

If you hide or delete a Crew List profile while keeping your Thalassa account, Thalassa retains a private safety marker containing only your account identifier and the time the marker was created. It remains until your Thalassa account is deleted and is used solely to prevent a former Crew List account from becoming eligible for unsolicited first-contact messages after its profile is removed. The marker is not public and contains no phone number, phone fingerprint, verification code, profile text, or photograph. Deleting your Thalassa account deletes the marker.

Crew List Profile Safety Check

When you ask to publish a Crew List profile, the profile fields intended for other approved members and each Crew List photograph are sent securely to Google Gemini for a limited content-safety classification. The check looks for a reasonable adult primary headshot, ordinary sailing or social content, scams, external contact details, advertising, and unsafe material. A clearly safe result may publish automatically. An uncertain or flagged result stays private for a Thalassa administrator to review; the automated check does not reject or suspend a member by itself.

Thalassa does not ask the safety service to identify you, match your face to any record, compare faces, perform a liveness check, create a facial template or embedding, or infer sensitive traits. This is content moderation, not identity verification. Thalassa retains a private, bounded audit record containing the decision, reason code, rules version, and cryptographic snapshots of the checked profile and file objects. That record does not copy the profile text, phone number, image bytes, or provider response. Your actual Crew List profile and photographs remain in Thalassa's private account storage until you edit or delete them or delete your account.

Location Data

Thalassa uses your device's GPS for:

  • Weather forecasts — to provide location-specific marine conditions
  • Anchor watch — continuous GPS monitoring while the feature is active
  • Diary entries — optional reverse-geocoded location tagging
  • Passage logging — GPS track recording during active voyages

Location is processed on-device and, when needed for a feature you request, may also be sent to Thalassa's cloud services and the applicable weather, mapping, geocoding, routing, or AI provider. While signed in, voyage tracks, diary/log positions, Guardian positions while armed, and other position-bearing records can sync privately to your account. Public or community sharing occurs only when you deliberately publish, hail, report, or share. You can disable location access through device settings, but location-dependent features will stop working.

Vessel & Operational Data

Data you enter into the App — including vessel specifications, log entries, diary notes, inventory items, maintenance records, equipment details, documents, plans, and crew information — is stored locally for offline operation. While you are signed in, supported account data syncs automatically over encrypted connections to Thalassa's Supabase infrastructure so it can be restored and used across your devices. Satellite mode may defer selected uploads; it is not a master cloud-sync opt-out.

NMEA Instrument Data

If you connect NMEA instruments, the live sentence stream is normally processed on-device. Derived readings and positions can be saved into diary entries, ship logs, tracks, diagnostics, or plans; those records may sync while signed in. Selected vessel context can also be sent to an AI service when you deliberately ask Calypso to use it. Thalassa does not continuously upload the raw NMEA stream as a general analytics feed.

Shared AIS (off by default)

Thalassa can contribute the AIS traffic your gateway hears to a shared fleet map and to AISHub. This is a separate, explicit opt-in, it is off unless you turn it on, and it is limited to AIS sentences — it does not change the statement above about your wider NMEA stream.

If your vessel transmits AIS, switching this on makes your vessel publicly trackable. Your MMSI, vessel name if programmed, position, course, and speed are broadcast on the public AIS network. Once AISHub receives them they are redistributed to member stations and other tracking sites within seconds. We cannot recall data that has already been redistributed. Switching sharing off stops further reports; it does not remove what has already been published. If your equipment only receives and never transmits, no information about your own vessel is published by this feature.

While sharing is on, Thalassa records a single contribution row for your account: total minutes your receiver was online, when we last heard from your device, a count of sentences sent, and your gateway's connection state. It does not record your position or any history of it — there is no location column and no per-check-in record, so your movements cannot be reconstructed from it. That row is deleted when your account is deleted, and is removed automatically after 400 days of inactivity. Contribution never affects what AIS, collision warnings, or vessel information you can see in the App.

AI, Voice & Optional Integrations

When you invoke AI or voice features, your prompt, audio or transcribed text, and the selected weather, vessel, passage, location, or app context are sent to the configured AI, speech-to-text, or text-to-speech provider. 3. Data Storage & Security

Local-First Architecture

Thalassa follows a local-first design: supported workflows remain available offline and queue changes for later delivery. Signed-in account data also syncs automatically to the cloud; local-first does not mean device-only.

  • Local storage: App databases, preferences, filesystem records, and IndexedDB are used for offline operation. Some authentication and integration credentials are stored in native app preferences; protect your unlocked device and disconnect integrations you no longer use.
  • Cloud storage: Synced data is stored on Supabase infrastructure with row-level security and is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Backups: Cloud-synced data is subject to Supabase's backup and disaster recovery policies
