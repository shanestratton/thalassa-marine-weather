Founding Skipper Applications

If you apply to test the public beta, we collect the name and email address you provide, your broad home-water region, boat type, Apple device, boating frequency, testing interests, optional notes, application source, and the date and version of your consent. We use this information only to assess the application, select a useful mix of testers, and contact you about the Thalassa beta. The form also uses a short-lived pseudonymous network identifier to limit abuse; Thalassa does not store your raw IP address with the application. Application details may also be processed by Resend, Thalassa's transactional email provider, solely to notify the operator about a new application and send transactional application receipt and acceptance emails to the address you provide. Operator notifications may arrive in the operator's private email inbox. Thalassa does not sell application data or use it for advertising.

Location Data

Thalassa uses your device's GPS for:

  • Weather forecasts — to provide location-specific marine conditions
  • Anchor watch — continuous GPS monitoring while the feature is active
  • Diary entries — optional reverse-geocoded location tagging
  • Passage logging — GPS track recording during active voyages

Location is processed on-device and, when needed for a feature you request, may also be sent to Thalassa's cloud services and the applicable weather, mapping, geocoding, routing, or AI provider. While signed in, voyage tracks, diary/log positions, Guardian positions while armed, and other position-bearing records can sync privately to your account. Public or community sharing occurs only when you deliberately publish, hail, report, or share. You can disable location access through device settings, but location-dependent features will stop working.

Vessel & Operational Data

Data you enter into the App — including vessel specifications, log entries, diary notes, inventory items, maintenance records, equipment details, documents, plans, and crew information — is stored locally for offline operation. While you are signed in, supported account data syncs automatically over encrypted connections to Thalassa's Supabase infrastructure so it can be restored and used across your devices. Satellite mode may defer selected uploads; it is not a master cloud-sync opt-out.

NMEA Instrument Data

If you connect NMEA instruments, the live sentence stream is normally processed on-device. Derived readings and positions can be saved into diary entries, ship logs, tracks, diagnostics, or plans; those records may sync while signed in. Selected vessel context can also be sent to an AI service when you deliberately ask Calypso to use it. Thalassa does not continuously upload the raw NMEA stream as a general analytics feed.

Shared AIS (off by default)

Thalassa can contribute the AIS traffic your gateway hears to a shared fleet map and to AISHub. This is a separate, explicit opt-in, it is off unless you turn it on, and it is limited to AIS sentences — it does not change the statement above about your wider NMEA stream.

If your vessel transmits AIS, switching this on makes your vessel publicly trackable. Your MMSI, vessel name if programmed, position, course, and speed are broadcast on the public AIS network. Once AISHub receives them they are redistributed to member stations and other tracking sites within seconds. We cannot recall data that has already been redistributed. Switching sharing off stops further reports; it does not remove what has already been published. If your equipment only receives and never transmits, no information about your own vessel is published by this feature.

While sharing is on, Thalassa records a single contribution row for your account: total minutes your receiver was online, when we last heard from your device, a count of sentences sent, and your gateway's connection state. It does not record your position or any history of it — there is no location column and no per-check-in record, so your movements cannot be reconstructed from it. That row is deleted when your account is deleted, and is removed automatically after 400 days of inactivity. Contribution never affects what AIS, collision warnings, or vessel information you can see in the App.

AI, Voice & Optional Integrations

When you invoke AI or voice features, your prompt, audio or transcribed text, and the selected weather, vessel, passage, location, or app context are sent to the configured AI, speech-to-text, or text-to-speech provider. Gmail and Apple Music connections are disabled in this public-beta candidate. If a future release enables one and you explicitly connect it, Thalassa will process the account tokens and content needed for the actions you request. You can disconnect an enabled integration in Settings and should also revoke its grant in the provider's account settings if you want the provider authorisation itself withdrawn.

Diagnostics

The beta may send crash reports and a small sample of performance traces to Sentry. These reports use a pseudonymous Thalassa account ID when you are signed in. We configure telemetry to omit your email address, precise location, request query strings, authentication data, and session replay. Local diagnostic logs remain on your device unless you choose to share them for support.

3. Data Storage & Security

Local-First Architecture

Thalassa follows a local-first design: supported workflows remain available offline and queue changes for later delivery. Signed-in account data also syncs automatically to the cloud; local-first does not mean device-only.

  • Local storage: App databases, preferences, filesystem records, and IndexedDB are used for offline operation. Some authentication and integration credentials are stored in native app preferences; protect your unlocked device and disconnect integrations you no longer use.
  • Cloud storage: Synced data is stored on Supabase infrastructure with row-level security and is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Backups: Cloud-synced data is subject to Supabase's backup and disaster recovery policies
