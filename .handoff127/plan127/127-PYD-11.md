departures, vision §9); it is cheap and it is what a skipper asks next.

## What the skipper sees

Shane's account (routing is owner-only in 127, decision 1), on a stop page
after "Route round the land" has routed it, one row under the route row:

- **A window, and her leave inside it:** "Leave 06:40–09:10 to carry the tide
  over the shallow bit 4.2 NM out, there and back; your 07:00 works."
- **Her leave outside it:** "Leave 08:20–10:30 to carry the tide over the
  shallow bit 4.2 NM out, there and back; your 07:00 is too early for it."
  (or "too late"). Tapping another leave chip re-says the last clause at
  once (memory only, no request).
- **Two openings in her day:** "Leave 06:40–09:10 or 18:20–19:00 to …".
- **More than one shallow bit:** "the shallow bits 1.1 and 4.2 NM out", "3
  shallow bits"; near an end: "leaving Coral Sea Marina", "at Cid Harbour"
  (within 0.5 NM of the start or the stop).
- **Nothing today clears it:** "No leave time today carries the tide over the
  shallow bit 4.2 NM out, there and back: see it on the chart, or try another
  day." (The vision's "the deep way round" is not offered: PYD routes one
  line, 'safest', and has no deeper alternative to point to. Saying one exists
  would be untrue.)
- **No tide data:** "Tide not known for the shallow bit 4.2 NM out (it needs
  0.9 m of tide): check the tide table before you go." Never a time.
- **Nothing to gate:** "No charted shallow water on the way: the tide doesn't
  limit when you leave."
- **Red the tide cannot clear** (a pin's dry tail, a stretch with no charted
  depth, red near a hazard): a second sentence, always: "Red on the route that
  no tide clears: see it on the chart." With it, "works" is never said alone.
- **Approximate curve:** "(tide approx)" after the times.
- **Overnight:** the window is for the way there; second sentence "Home
  tomorrow: check its tide in the morning."
- **Footnote** (the stop page's own, once): "Tide from WorldTides
  ({station}); check the official tide table."
- When routed, the "Leaving a marina: check its approach depth against the
  tide before you go." row (today.ts:2086) is not shown: the routed line went
  through the approach on her charts and this row says when the tide allows
  it.

Testers see none of it (no routed line exists for them in 127). The weather
verdict, the stop's level and the "best" leave never change because of it:
tide changes when, never whether (the sweep's own doctrine,
DepartureSweepInshore.ts:10-13; Cast Off is advisory).

## Today (b127 719645bb7)

- **The sweep exists and is pure.** `sweepDepartures`
  services/routing/DepartureSweepInshore.ts:101-240: N departures from
  `startMs` at `stepMs`, each walked by `annotateRoute`
  (services/routing/TideAwareAnnotator.ts:204) at ONE constant `SpeedModel`
  (`stwMs()`, env/EnvFields.ts:93-96), each shallow spot's ETA gated by

  TideField → every gated departure 'unknown', "never a guess, never a

## Decision (our recommendation)

1. **One pure module, services/dayPlanner/leaveWindow.ts**, two functions:
   - `leaveSpots(coords, engine)`: the gated runs (finite `minDepthM`, not
     `dryTail`; a needs-tide pin tail IS gated), grouped by
     `tideCurveBucket(minAtLat ?? midLat, minAtLon ?? midLon)`, each group
     turned into `ShallowSpot`s by `shallowRunsToDepartureSpots(coords,
     groupRuns)`; for each run its along-track NM from the start (cumulative
     haversine to the spot's leg) for the words; plus `redNoTide: boolean` =
     any `dryTail`, any run with `minDepthM === null`, `partUncharted`,
     `nearHazard` or `chartsDisagree`, or a non-empty
     `dangerWithoutChartedDepth(engine)`; plus `needM` per run (draft + 0.5 −
     minDepth) for the "tide not known" words.
   - `leaveWindow({ coords, spots, departures, stay, curves, chosenMs, zone,
     cruiseKts })` → `{ kind: 'window' | 'none' | 'unknown' | 'not-gated',
     windows: {fromMs, toMs}[] (≤ 2), chosen: 'works' | 'too-early' |
     'too-late' | null, approx, redNoTide, where, needM? }`.
2. **The sweep, there and back.** For each hourly `DepartureEval` d, one
   `sweepDepartures` call per tide group with `startMs = d.departureMs − 30
   min` (never before the day's earliest leave), `count = 6`, `stepMs = 10
   min`, `speed = nm / d.out.durationMs` (cruising speed when null), `tide`
   = that group's curve, `draftM` = the draft 2 routed with. Day trip: the same
   for the way home on the reversed line (leg index mirrored: `last −
   legIndex`), `startMs = step + (d.stayEndMs − d.departureMs)`, speed `nm /
   d.home.durationMs`. Each group's `TideCurve` is wrapped with
   `tideFieldFromCurve` (services/routing/env/EnvFields.ts, as
   components/passage/DepartureSweepSheet.tsx:206 does): `sweepDepartures`
   takes a `TideField`, not a curve. A step is clear only if every group is clear both ways;
   unknown if any is unknown and none blocked; blocked otherwise. Contiguous
   clear steps make a window; at most two windows are said. Overnight: the way
   there only.
3. **Tide curves: the router's own, no new budget.** New
   `TodayLoaderDeps.loadRouteTideCurve(lat, lon, fromMs, toMs)` =
   `fetchTideCurve(lat, lon, fromMs, toMs, { days: TIDE_CURVE_MAX_DAYS })`
   under `withTimeout` 12 s (as the chips). One call per group bucket, at most
   `ROUTE_TIDE_CURVES_MAX` (4); a group past the cap or whose curve fails is
   'unknown' ("tide not known there"). It normally hits the cache the routing
   just filled. The fixture fakes it.
4. **When it runs.** Once, when 2's result arrives routed and the page is
   still open (after the paint, never in the same tick as the A*); the
   curves are fetched once per routed stop; changing the leave chip re-runs
   only the `chosen` clause (pure, no sweep). Kept beside the route in
   TodaySheet's `routes` map; dropped with it (2's rules: page closed, start
   changed, sheet closed, account changed).
5. **Words** exactly as "What the skipper sees"; times by `hhmm(ms, zone)`
   (today.ts:235) on the place's clock; "there and back" only for a day trip;
   "(tide approx)" if any group's curve is `EXTREMES_INTERP`; the red
   sentence whenever `redNoTide`; never a time when `unknown`; never "works"
   alone when `redNoTide`. An unverified route (`engine.stateMask === null`,
   where `dangerWithoutChartedDepth` returns null) gets NO leave row: 2's row
   already says "not verified: recalculate in Auto", and a window over a line
   whose masks did not verify would be false confidence.
6. **Licence and terms (common rules 3 and 7).** Memory only: the window, the
   curves' use and the needed rise are never written to storage, the account
   or a log line (no log line at all in this package). The window is derived
   from charted depths, so it is on screen only (C10). WorldTides: shown to
   her only, "check the official tide table" in the footnote (their terms: not
   the only source for navigation), never on a shared surface (no day card in
   127). Credited with the station name when the curve has one
   (TideHeightService.ts:86 `stationName`).
7. **Nothing else moves.** The level, the reason, `best`, the ranking and
   the card never read the window (tide changes timing, not preference).

## Not in this build

- Moving the "best" leave time to the tide, or marking leave chips that miss
  it (the row says it; chips stay as they are until 128's stop page, PYD-8).
- Waiting at anchor short of the bar ("leave now, wait 40 min off X"): the
  sweep's v1 has no in-passage waiting (DepartureSweepInshore.ts:17-20).
- Currents in the ETA (CMEMS is ETA-only and held, DepartureSweepSheet.tsx:
  8-11).
- The way home tomorrow for an overnight (said, not checked).
- The window on the ribbon (6) or the day card (130, open sources only).
- Routing a deeper alternative when nothing clears (one line, 'safest').

## Risks

1. **A window that is wrong in the water.** Charted minimums, WorldTides
   heights (approximate where interpolated), a constant speed per departure
   and the 0.5 m margin; the edges are already pulled in 30 min. The words say
   "check the official tide table", and the red sentence stops a false
   "works". Shane checks the first ones against his own tide table.
2. **Dry stretches it cannot see** (named only in Auto's sentences): the red
   sentence fires on every field that marks red the tide cannot clear; Auto's
   chart (3) names them.
3. **Cost on the phone.** ~170 short `annotateRoute` walks over a few hundred
   vertices; measured in vitest; it runs after the route has painted.
4. **Fit.** One more row on the stop page; the marina row it replaces pays for
   most of it; the Playwright fit at 572 px is the test. 127 adds a verdict
   row (1), a why line (4), the owner timing line (2) and this row before
   PYD-8's restructure (128) takes rows away. **If the longest case (a
   reviewed stop with Local notes, routed, two windows, red sentence) does
   not fit at 375x667 as drawn (critic pass 2), fold in this order:** the
   owner timing line joins the route row ("… · 6.4 s"); the overnight /
   red second sentence moves under a "more" disclosure in the same row; never
   the main button or the leave row's first sentence into a scroll.
