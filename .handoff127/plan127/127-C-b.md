## What the skipper sees

Almost nothing changes on screen aboard, and that is the point: depths, marks, tide windows and reasons are all still
shown, worked out in memory. What changes is what is **kept** on the phone, in the account and in files:

- **Route check, after a relaunch.** A route she checked yesterday opens with its legs already coloured (green,
  amber, red, "needs tide"), with no re-check at launch, as today. Over licensed charts, a leg's reason is no longer
  kept, so its row reads e.g. "caution · tap for why" and a "needs tide" leg reads "needs tide · tap for the window".
  Tapping the row, opening the Route report or pressing Save re-checks those legs on her charts and the full words
  come back ("thin water: 1.6 m charted at low tide"). A leg that crosses land still says "crosses charted land"
  from the kept words, and can never be acknowledged. In NOAA (US) waters nothing changes at all.
- **Saved routes (Auto, and Plan Your Day's Save once PYD-3 turns it on).** Save works as today. What is kept with an
  autorouted route over licensed charts is the line, each leg's grade, the planned-only words and her draft. The
  chart notes ("water charted 1.2 m", mark positions, leading-line offsets) are shown while she reviews and are not
  stored: the saved route says "Chart notes for this route were shown when it was worked out and aren't kept. Open
  it on the chart aboard to check it again."
- **Voyage plans and the Log.** An inshore plan worked out on licensed charts keeps its line, its distance and
  number-free notes. Its notes in the Log read, e.g., "⚠ Red on this route: part of it dries or has water no tide
  clears for your boat. Open it on the chart aboard to see where." in place of "the Boat Passage dries 2.2 m and you
  need 2.9 m". The "follow anyway" red finding still fires from that line. Opened again after a relaunch, the
  departure planner says "Tide gates for this route need your boat's charts: recalculate it aboard to sweep
  departures against them." It never treats the route as tide-clear because the gates weren't kept.
- **Route report PDF.** Over licensed charts the PDF prints each leg's grade and "needs tide" with times, not
  charted depths, mark names or reasons. A footer says "Depths and marks are shown aboard in Thalassa; licensed chart
  figures aren't printed." GPX export (her line) is unchanged.
- **AvNav o-charts pictures stay on the Pi** (decision 11). Nothing on screen changes: the control that turned an
  AvNav chart on has been parked since 18 July, and her o-charts draw as vector cells from the Pi. Open AvNav
  charts (mbtiles, gemf) are unchanged.
- Settings, Plan Your Day, the desk map: unchanged by this package.

## Risks

1. **A stub that hides danger.** Covered three ways: the land words are kept, a stub never releases, and a stub is
   re-checked on the skipper's tap, report or save. Tested.
2. **Fingerprint drift.** If `licence` leaked into `encCellContentIdentity`, every saved check would expire at once.
   A test pins the fingerprint string.
3. **A reader silently treating a stripped plan as clear.** The allow-list is paired with an explicit
   `chartFacts: 'aboard-only'` marker that every reader of the dropped fields checks first; the sweep test pins it.
4. **Mirror collision with C-d.** Both may re-sync the router mirror; this package merges first, C-d re-runs
   `--check` at staging.
5. **sw.js conflict with 127-H** (its mapbox age cap edits :665-705, beside this deletion). This package merges
   first; 127-H rebases. If 127-H's sw.js half is cut to 128, this deletion still ships. **This package is the one
   owner of the LAN-tile deletion** (127-C-c's coordination note pointing it at 127-H is superseded).
6. **The crash-loop guard under 127-C-c (critic HIGH, passes 2 and 3). The fix is designed in 127-C-c
   decision 7a.**
   - The hydrate (useTracerGrading.ts:226-234) is one-shot and compares the whole-library
     `getRegistryFingerprint()`. Under C-c the protected registry is memory-only and fills from the Pi
     after launch.
   - The bank is a single payload per account, rewritten after every cluster (:335) and at the end of each
     pass (:346). So a pass graded before registration also **overwrites** yesterday's v5 bank, with a
     fingerprint that lacks the boat's cells.
   - Registration rides the auto-sync, which defers while the tracer is open (autoSyncFromPi.ts:95-98).
   - 7a's answer:
     - `ensureBoatRegistry()` is called by the tracer itself and never deferred by plotting;
     - while 'pending' there is no hydrate, no grade and no persist, and the line "Opening Serene Summer's
       charts from the Pi…" shows;
     - the hydrate is keyed by fingerprint, not one-shot, and re-runs on the transition to 'loaded';
     - when away, verdicts are provisional: never persisted, never releasable;
     - the background check waits the same way.
   - **This package's part:** the v5 stubs that the hydrate finds, and the fail-closed persist rule above.
     The registry-pending tests (paired + pending → zero legs graded; registration → hydrate hits with zero
     regrades; jetsam reload → zero graded) live in C-c's test 13, because the gate first exists there.
     This package's `tracerAutoBank` remount case (zero legs graded with stubs persisted) must hold first.
