## What the skipper sees

- **A small map on each stop:** the hero (about 96-120 px), each alternate
  (40 px) and the stop page (full width, ~180-200 px). Land is drawn from the
  OpenStreetMap coastline Plan Your Day already fetches; the water is the
  sheet's surface; a dot at the start, a pin at the stop in its level colour,
  and a downwind arrow badge with "SE 14 g21" on the big one, plus a scale bar
  ("5 NM").
- **The route on it, once routed:** the router's own line, coloured as Auto
  colours it (green, amber where it needs tide, red where it dries). A stop not
  routed yet shows no line at all and says "Not routed yet" on the big picture.
  Never a straight line.
- **A shelter rose** beside the shelter word: a 20 px circle with the bearings
  that have land close by filled, and the wind arrow across it, so "Very
  sheltered" shows where the shelter is. Dashed ring when shelter is not known.
- Outside Queensland the land layer may be empty (no coastline loaded): the
  picture then shows water, the marks and the line only, and says
  "Coastline not loaded".

## Decision (our recommendation)

1. **Keep the coastline on TodayBase** (`coastline: readonly Segment[] |
   null`), the same in-memory array coastlineSource already holds for the
   session (no copy, no new storage).
2. **`services/dayPlanner/thumb.ts` (pure):** for a stop, the bbox of start,
   stop and route + 15% (square, min 2 NM); prefilter segments by bbox (one
   pass), clip, join into rings where they connect, Douglas-Peucker to ~1 px at
   the target size; memoised per (stop, size bucket). Target ≤ 15 ms per stop
   on the Mac for the Whitsundays coastline; computed only for visible stops.
3. **`components/dayPlanner/StopThumb.tsx`:** one SVG; land path; start dot;
   stop pin; route pieces from 2's in-memory proposal via
   `inshoreRoutePieces` (the same function Auto's chart uses, imported
   dynamically); wind badge and scale bar on the big size; `role="img"` with an
   aria label ("Map: Airlie Bay to Cid Harbour, routed 16.5 NM, 0.3 NM needs
   tide").
4. **`components/dayPlanner/ShelterRose.tsx`:** 36 wedges from `fetchLandNM`
   (filled where land ≤ 2 NM, fainter to 5 NM), the wind arrow across it; the
   word stays beside it and in the aria.
5. **No WebGL, no Mapbox** (2 GB WebContent cap; one map instance is enough in
   the app).
6. **Licence:** land = OSM only (credit already in the line: "Places ©
   OpenStreetMap"; becomes "Map data and places © OpenStreetMap"). The route
   polyline and its colours come from the in-memory proposal and are never
   cached: no image, no data URL, no stored path. No chart-cell geometry is
   ever read for a picture.
7. **Tiers (127-PYD-7 decision 5):** pictures in the full-hero tier only
   (740 px tall and up): hero 96-120 px, alternates 40 px. The compact hero
   (600-739 px, and below 600) has no thumbnail: it is ≤ 84 px. Large text
   hides the alternates' thumbnails.
   **The stop page's picture needs its own height rule (critic 2026-10-10).**
   The sheet's room is 483 / 572 / 685 / 754 px at 320x568 / 375x667 /
   390x844 / 430x932 (day-planner-layout.spec.ts:44-52). The mock's panel C
   used 626 of 683 px at 390 x 779 with a ~160 px picture, i.e. ~466 px of
   page without it; 127-PYD-11 adds a leave row (~36-54 px) and 2 the owner's
   timing line, so the page without a picture is ~500-520 px. At 572 px that
   leaves ~50-70 px: no useful picture. So the height is computed from the
   MEASURED page, never fixed: `pictureH = clamp(0, room − pageWithoutPicture
   − 16, 200)`, shown only when ≥ 96 px (below that the route row's words
   carry it). Expected: none at 320x568 and 375x667, ~150 px at 390x844, ~200
   px at 430x932. Never push the timeline, the leave row or the main button
   into a scroll for a picture.
