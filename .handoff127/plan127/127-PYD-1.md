## Decision (our recommendation)

1. `StopRow` gains `line2Reason: string | null`: for level 'over' | 'unknown'
   with a reason, line 2 shows `⚡ ` + reason for thunder, otherwise the reason
   with its prefix stripped and a capital ("Home after dark (18:28)",
   "SE 30 kn on the way" under the ✕ glyph which already means over). Times stay
   on Inside/Near rows only.
2. One reason vocabulary: reasons are stored WITHOUT the "over your limits: "
   prefix; the prefix is added once where words need it (aria, Not today list,
   detail first row). Update every producer (planStop :1517-1531,
   areaOverReason :651-655, preRank's lightMissReason, areaVerdict).
3. `dayHeadline` thunder: when the first part after the window is Near BECAUSE
   of thunder (part.thunder >= 2), the "… gets near your limits." clause is
   replaced by "Thunder from about HH:MM in N of M models." On an all-inside or
   all-near day with thunder anywhere, add the same clause once. Never a second
   clause; no longer than today's longest template (the "thunder" fixture
   headline is the length test).
4. `partCell` glyph '⚡' when thunder drives Near; word stays 'Thunder'.
5. `DayChip` gains `best: PartLevel` (dayParts for that date, already computed
   for `lighter`, today.ts:1906-1910); the chip shows PART_GLYPH[best] in the
   level colour; the aria keeps the agreement words. Headline adds "Models
   differ a little." for 'some' on the shown day.
6. Aria: `${STOP_ARIA[level]}: ${reason}` with the prefix-free reason; skip
   the fallback when line 2 already says "weather not checked".
7. Offline headline words (today.ts:797).
8. CSS: `.today-primary` #0e7490 in dark; `.today-tag` amber outline
   (#fbbf24 border, transparent fill, #fcd34d text; light: #b45309).
9. Icons from components/Icons.tsx; text "Local notes"; "boat 2 h ago".
10. Drop the duplicated landing note; `shortCredit` lists only sources that
    answered.
