## Decision (our recommendation)

**Commit 1: places**
1. A second, separately cached Overpass query per 1° cell (same cache and 30-day
   policy as the anchorage cells; only the cell leaves the phone, never the
   position): `nwr["natural"="bay"]["name"](bbox); out center 3001;` with a
   25 s server timeout. Bays whose name matches the atlas nav filter
   (`/passage|channel|sound|\bpass\b|flats/i`) are dropped, as the QLD build
   does. Not asked inside the QLD atlas tiles (the atlas already has them).
2. Bays become places with `source: 'osm-bay'`, label "Mapped bay · not
   reviewed", the same 1 NM / reach / no-anchoring / closure rules, and a land
   table from the coastline when it is loaded.
3. Seamark anchorage areas are kept at `out center` with `approximate: true`
   and the stop-page note "area: pick your spot"; distance and plans use the
   centre.
4. Unnamed anchorages take the nearest named bay or named feature within 2 NM:
   "Anchorage off {name}".
5. Start name: nearest named place (bay, anchorage, marina) within 5 NM ->
   "Near {name}"; else coordinates.
6. Load: bays add one query per cell (measured 10-24 s, sometimes timing out);
   places show as they arrive (`placesStatus` 'loading' -> 'ok'/'partial'),
   as the anchorage cells do today. With up to `MAX_REFERENCE_TILES` = 4 cells
   that doubles a load to 8 Overpass queries against one mirror that already
   answered 429/504 to 6 of ~20 calls: run the bay queries AFTER the anchorage
   cells, at most 2 at a time, never in parallel with them (critic
   2026-10-10). A 429 on a bay cell is 'partial', never a blank list.

**Commit 2: shelter without the freeze**
7. A uniform grid index over the coastline segments (built once per coastline,
   ~0.01° cells) so each of the 36 rays tests only the cells it crosses;
   `assessFetch` keeps its signature and results (same numbers to 1e-9 on the
   fixtures).
8. `gatherPlaces` ray-casts in chunks: at most ~16 ms of work, then yield
   (`await` a resolved paint), so the main thread is never held > 50 ms. Target:
   the La Trinité case (224 points, 103k segments) ≤ 300 ms total on the Mac.
   **Ray-cast only what survives (critic 2026-10-10):** today every OSM point
   in up to `MAX_REFERENCE_TILES` = 4 one-degree cells is ray-cast inside the
   OSM loop (places.ts:501-523), BEFORE the reach, 1 NM, no-anchoring and
   same-name filters; points 60 NM away pay for a table nobody reads. Move the
   ray-cast after those filters (and after 4's likelyAnchorage filter). That
   alone may cut La Trinité's 7.8 s by more than the index does; measure both.
   Chunking makes `gatherPlaces` async: say so in its signature and update
   todayLoader's caller and the tests that call it synchronously.
9. Coastline fetch for Plan Your Day: per-call timeout 20 s (other callers keep
   9 s), in the background: places and plans show first with "Shelter not
   known", then update when the coastline lands.
10. No new Overpass mirror in 127: a new operator would see coastline boxes
    around the boat; it needs a privacy review first. kumi stays listed (it may
    recover). **128:** our own coastline-and-places proxy (cache by coarse
    cell, the boat's position never reaches a third party, and web gets
    shelter too; it is server work and a deploy).

## Risks

1. Bays are not anchorages: some dry, some are rocky or exposed. Labelled "not
   reviewed", pin depth when charted (4), and shelter only from the land table.
2. More Overpass load and slower first answers in new places (bays take 10-24
   s). Cached per cell for 30 days; the sheet never waits on them.
3. Shared code: shelterGeometry and coastlineSource also serve Anchorage
   Tonight and The Glass's shelter; the equality test protects them.
