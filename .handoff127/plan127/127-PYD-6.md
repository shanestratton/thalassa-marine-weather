## What the skipper sees

The three identical tiles ("≈ SE 14 / Near" x3, ~145 px saying one thing) and
the light-and-tide line become one picture of her day:

- **A ribbon from first to last light**, split into Morning / Afternoon /
  Evening, each segment filled in its level colour WITH its word and glyph inside
  ("✓ MORNING", "≈ AFTERNOON"; never colour alone). Thunder in two or more
  models is hatched over the segment with "⚡". Past and dark parts are grey.
- **Her trip drawn on it:** for the card's first stop, a dot at leave, a bar
  while ashore, a ring at home, on the same time scale. She sees at once whether
  the trip sits inside the good part of the day.
- **Under it:** first light and last light at the ends, HW/LW ticks with times
  where they fall in daylight, and under each part a small downwind arrow and
  "SE 14·21" (wind·gust).
- **A short verdict line above it:** "✓ Go early, home by 12:00" with
  "⚡ thunder from noon" or "models: some spread" set to the right; the full
  sentence (from 1) stays as the VoiceOver text and is what the compact tier
  shows when the ribbon is folded.
- **Day chips that say something:** each chip has two lines, the day and its
  best level ("Today / ≈ Near", "Sat / ✓ Lighter" when it is the lighter day).
  The stay control sits apart after a divider, with a clock line icon and
  "2 h ▾", so it stops reading as a fourth day.
- Light theme and dark theme from the sheet's own level tokens (dark
  #4ade80 / #fbbf24 / #f87171; light #15803d / #b45309 / #b91c1c; slate for ?).

## Decision (our recommendation)

1. **Pure model first.** `ribbonModel(view, base, nowMs)` in a new
   services/dayPlanner/ribbon.ts: segments {part, startMs, endMs, level,
   glyph, word, thunder}, light ends, tide ticks (daylight only), wind per part
   {dirDeg, kts, gust}, trip {leaveMs, arriveMs, stayEndMs, homeMs} from
   `view.top[0].plan.best` (overnight: leave and arrive only). Polar day:
   06:00-20:00 with "Light all day"; no daylight: no ribbon (headline says so);
   offline: grey "No forecast" segments with light and cached tides.
2. **One SVG, no library**, `components/dayPlanner/DayRibbon.tsx`: a
   `viewBox` scaled to the card width; text in SVG `<text>` at the sheet's room
   clamps (CSS custom properties); the hatch is an SVG pattern. A narrow segment
   shortens its word ("EVE") then shows the glyph only, with the full word in the
   accessible list.
3. **Accessibility.** The SVG is `aria-hidden`; a visually hidden `<ul
   aria-label="The day">` keeps the three `partCell` aria labels (so VoiceOver
   steps through parts as today) plus the facts aria. The verdict line keeps
   `aria-live="polite"` with the full sentence.
4. **Headline.** `dayHeadline` returns `{ short, text, side? }`: `short` ≤ 32
   characters ("Go early, home by 12:00", "Near your limits all day",
   "Stay put today", "Inside your limits all day"), `side` = "⚡ thunder from
   noon" | "models: some spread" | "models split" | none; `text` = today's full
   sentence. Tests pin every template.
5. **Chips.** `DayChip.best` from 1; two-line chip; stay control apart with a
   divider and ClockIcon. 44 px minimum targets (126-17b sizes them up to 52).
6. **Wind arrow** drawn locally (a 12-line SVG path); The Glass is not touched.
7. **Removed:** the tiles' markup and CSS, `factsLine`'s visible use (kept for
   aria and Sources). That is the trim this package brings to the budget.
8. **Fit.** The ribbon block is ≤ the tiles + facts height it replaces at every
   room (target ~84-100 px at 430 as drawn vs ~110 today); the floors keep the
   SE at today's sizes or smaller. At 375x667 as drawn the sheet's room is 572
   px (common, Layout rules): the ribbon must be ≤ 76 px there, so 7's hero
   and alternates keep the room they are budgeted (7, decision 5).
