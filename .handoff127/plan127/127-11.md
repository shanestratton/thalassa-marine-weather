## What the skipper sees

**On the chart (Obs), phone, iPad and desktop web:**
- A round 48 px **north arrow** sits with the zoom buttons on the right-hand rail (placement under Decision 6).
  Its red needle always points at true north on screen. Under it, the mode in plotter shorthand: **N UP**,
  **C UP**, **T UP**, **H UP**. VoiceOver reads it in words ("Chart orientation: Track up, turned to 047
  degrees. Tap for North up").
- **One tap when the chart is turned puts it North up**, at once, with no menu.
- **One tap when it is North up opens "Chart orientation"**: a centred card with four big rows. Each row says
  what it uses and whether it is ready now:

  | Row | Line under it (examples) |
  |---|---|
  | North up | "North at the top, like a paper chart" |
  | Course up | "The route ahead at the top · Airlie → Cid Harbour" / "Needs a route on the chart and her position" |
  | Track up | "Her course over the ground at the top · 047°" / "Waits until she is making 1.5 kn" |
  | Heading up | "Her bow at the top, from her compass · 052°T" / "Needs a true heading from her compass" / "Her compass is jumping between two headings" |

  Any row can be picked. A mode that cannot be honoured yet keeps the chart North up, shows a small amber dot
  on the arrow, and turns the chart the moment its data arrives (advice, not walls).
- **The chart turns gently.** It eases to each new heading or course over about a second, ignores the small
  wander of a boat holding her course, and turns about her when she is on screen, so she stays where she is
  while the chart swings round her. It never turns while a finger is on the chart or while the camera is
  already flying somewhere (Locate, Show on map); it catches up the moment that ends.
- **What turns and what stays upright:** the chart, its depths, light sectors, leads, AIS boats (each pointing
  her real way), wind barbs and the wind streaks all turn together and stay true. Every word stays upright:
  place names, soundings, the boat's "Stopped" / "6.2 kts" badge, her wind chip, AIS names, the SART ⊗.
- **Honest limits, said where it matters:** on Hybrid and on OpenSeaMap seamarks the names are part of the
  picture, so they turn with the chart; vector names (Relief, ENC, place names) stay upright. Her compass
  reaches the app every 2 s through the Pi and every 5 s straight from the gateway, so Heading up turns in
  smoothed steps, not like a plotter's 10 Hz.
- The choice is remembered on this device only, separately for the chart and the planner.

**On the planner (the tracer, phone and desktop web):** the tracer's compass rose (top-left, always shown while
tracing) becomes the control. Tap it: **North up** or **Course up on this leg**, which turns the chart so the
selected pin's leg (else the first leg) points up. Pick another pin and the chart turns to that leg: a
leg-by-leg briefing of which side of each mark she passes. The rose already counter-rotates
(CompassRoseOverlay.tsx:33-46), so it stays honest. Track and Heading up are not offered while planning.

**Plan Your Day:** its routed line opens in Auto's chart (127-PYD-3), which stays North up: a one-route review
chart, locked like today (AutoroutingTrialWorkspace.tsx:378, :405). "Open on the main chart" puts it in the
tracer, where Course up on this leg works, and Follow route puts it on Obs for Course up underway.

## Decision (our recommendation)

### 1. The modes, exactly
| Mode | Target bearing | Steadiness | When it cannot be honoured |
|---|---|---|---|
| North up | 0 | - | - |
| Course up (Obs) | The route ahead: from the point abeam of her to the point 0.3 NM further along | Held. A new value replaces it only after it differs by ≥ 10° for 5 s (a leg change), so a dense autorouted line's small bends never turn the chart | No route on the chart, or no position: North up + reason. Past the end: the last leg |
| Course up (planner) | The leg from the selected pin, else the first leg | Changes only when the selection changes | Fewer than two pins: North up |
| Track up | Her COG, circular EMA τ 8 s | Turns at ≥ 6° change, at most once per 4 s | Below 1.0 kn for 20 s: North up; resumes at ≥ 1.5 kn for 5 s. Sources: her lanes; **the phone's GPS course when the phone is all she has** (most punters worldwide have no gateway) |
| Heading up | Her TRUE heading only, circular EMA τ 3 s | Turns at ≥ 4° change, at most once per 2 s | No fresh true heading, or the held lane: North up + "Needs a true heading from her compass". Never `heading` (legacy), never the phone compass, never COG |

**Two-compass guard (Heading up).** Over the last 60 s, two or more A→B→A swings of ≥ 15° between consecutive
samples, while she is stopped (SOG < 0.5 kn) or her COG holds within 5°, mean two sources, not a turning boat.
Heading up then shows North up with "Her compass is jumping between two headings", and clears after 60 s steady.
A real tack moves the COG too, so it never trips this.

All angles use shortest-arc maths (350° → 10° passes 0°). Mapbox's own `_normalizeBearing` already eases the
short way.

### 2. One owner of the bearing
- `components/map/chartOrientation.ts` (new) owns the mode, the target and the turn. Nothing else sets a bearing
  on the chart.
- It turns with `map.easeTo({ bearing, duration: 12 ms per degree within 300-1200 ms, easing: ease-out, around:
  her position when she is on screen }, { thalassaOrientation: true })`. Mapbox applies `around` to bearing
  changes too (easeTo, mapbox-gl-unminified.js:84858-84884).
- **It never turns** while `map.isMoving()`, while a pointer is down on the chart (and 300 ms after), or while
  Obs is hidden (display:none or the app in the background: no listeners, no timers, as CloseInWindLayer does).
  The latest target is applied once at the next `moveend`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: duration 0.
- Fits read `chartFitBearing(map)` (A8), so a fit and the mode always agree and the overview cannot ping-pong.
- **Her motion comes from the marker.** useVesselTracker publishes what it already resolved (lane, position,
  instruments, the lane's max age) to a tiny module store (`ownshipMotion`). The controller applies its own
  per-mode rules with the freshness helpers exported from ownshipDirection.ts. Same lanes, same gates, same
  "never borrow a vessel compass on the phone's fix".
- **Per-frame work stays out of MapHub.** The arrow button self-subscribes to 'rotate' and repaints its needle
  through rAF, the ZoomLevelFab pattern (ZoomLevelFab.tsx:1-15). MapHub never holds the bearing in state.
- **Flight recorder crumbs** on mode and fallback changes only (`chart:orient track→north slow`), never a
  coordinate. No new log lines.
- No native plugin, no phone compass, nothing new on the Capacitor bridge queue (memory capacitor-bridge-queue).

### 3. Keep her on screen in the turning modes (Shane question 1)
- In Course, Track and Heading up only: when she is on screen and making way, and she leaves the middle 60% of
  the view, the camera eases her back to the lower third (one move, at most every 20 s). Locate in those modes
  puts her in the lower third too (`flyTo` with a screen offset).
- Any pan or pinch stands it down until Locate is tapped. Any camera claim (obsCameraClaims.ts: Locate, Show on
  map) stands it down the same way. North up never moves the camera on its own, as today.
- Built behind one constant. Until Shane answers, on in the turning modes only.

### 4. Gestures stay locked, and the keyboard hole closes

### 7. Wind field, exactly (A1)
- While a turning mode is on, the Leaflet div becomes a square of side ⌈view diagonal⌉, centred on the
  container (the map container already clips). Turning in or out of a mode re-sizes it once
  (`invalidateSize`; the field re-seeds once). North up keeps today's `inset:0` div exactly.
- The transform about the div centre O is `translate(T) rotate(−B) scale(s)`, with
  T = project(anchor) − O − R(−B)·s·r, where r is where Leaflet drew the anchor relative to O. At s = 1 and
  B = 0 this is today's formula. `trackCamera` applies it every move frame (rotation included), `syncFull` at
  each settle. Leaflet's field stays north-up inside the div, so its streak directions come out true on screen.
- Particle multiplier: the same visible density up to 1.6× today's particle count, thinner beyond it (a
  portrait phone's square is about 2.6× its area); `particleScale` still applies on top. The device smoke
  checks frame rate (PerfOverlay). Canvas memory about +2 MB at most.
- Coordinate with 127-DESKMAP-b (the desktop wind option edits this file): merge after it. **DESKMAP-b's
  rotation stopgap** (fade the field and say "Wind streaks show with North up" when the bearing is not 0;
  DESKMAP-b decision 10, behind a constant) is superseded here: 11a deletes it and its test, and A2's close-in
  rotation is this package's, not DESKMAP-b's (critic pass 2: the two plans had promised opposite behaviour).

## Risks
- **Camera fights.** Any new camera code that forgets the helper snaps the chart north. Mitigations: the
  behavioural ping-pong test, the source guard, and the controller re-applying its target after every moveend
  (a stray north snap self-heals within a second).
- **Moveend churn in Track up** (wind refetch, ENC, AIS re-clip). Mitigations: deadbands and minimum intervals
  (at most one turn per 2-4 s and none while holding course); the bearing-invariant ENC window; wind's covering
  cache.
- **The wind overlay's extra particles** while turned (capped at 1.6×). Measured in the device smoke.
- **Her two compasses.** If the guard is too eager, a real swing at anchor in a gust could read as two sources;
  it needs reversal and a steady COG or a stopped boat, and it only ever falls back to North up.
- **Course up off the route** (far off the line): it still shows the leg ahead. That is what plotters do; the
  passage pane already shows her offset.
