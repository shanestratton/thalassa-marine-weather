## Decision (our recommendation)

1. `DayPlanInput.opened?: ReadonlySet<string>`: ids she opened this session.
   `planDay` adds them to `needsLegs` (after the five route-checked stops) and
   plans them with their legs when they arrive. Opening is the only trigger: no
   background sweep of the list.
2. `view.rows: ReadonlyMap<string, StopRow>` for every planned place;
   TodaySheet's `detailRow` uses it (with 4's pin rule). AllPlaces uses it.
3. AllPlaces: every row that has a plan is a StopButton (`compact` prop: one
   line of times, no tag); excluded and closed places stay text; sections sorted
   by `straightNm`; headers as above; the area-wind basis said once per section.
4. Opening a "Not today" place: its page leads with the reason (1's first row),
   then times on the area wind or the route.
5. Request budget: at most 2 proxy calls per newly opened place, once per place
   per start (the `requested` set), plus 2 more only if the owner taps Route
   and it routes (2's routed legs). Opening never routes.
6. Line 2 for area-wind rows: the times, with "(area wind)" only in the header
   and the aria label, not on every row.
