# 127-01 Shore Watch: no Leave button on the skipper's own boat

**Authority.** Shane 2026-10-10 ~15:15, verbatim: "ok on the anchor watch, can we remove the Leave button. not sure what it is suppose to do. but it works identical to weigh anchor". Standing order: "your recommendations for all work requiring an answer". This reverses part of his 2026-09-29 choice (memory thalassa-anchor-watch-one-truth: "Weigh anchor + Leave in Shore Watch for his own Pi"), at his request.

## Today (master bd2d35b07)

- components/AnchorWatchPage.tsx, shore view (viewMode === 'shore', ~:1508 onward):
  - `ownPiWatch` (~:1510) is true when the Shore Watch is this phone's own Pi's watch.
  - The PageHeader `action` (~:1633-1641) is a red **Leave** button (aria-label "Leave Shore Watch") calling `handleStopWatch` (~:677). In shore mode that only calls `AnchorWatchSyncService.leaveSession()` and returns to setup; the Pi keeps watching.
  - When `ownPiWatch`, `ShoreWeighAnchorBar` (~:1758) offers **Weigh anchor** → `handleWeighAnchorFromShore` (~:732): `AnchorPiWatchKeeper.end()` (stops renewals, asks the Pi to stop) then `leaveSession()`.
  - Both land on the setup view, so to the skipper they look identical. Leave adds nothing the header's back arrow (`onBack`) does not, and invites the wrong tap next to Weigh anchor.

## Decision (our recommendation, on Shane's ask)

1. **Own boat (`ownPiWatch`): no Leave button.** The header shows no action (or nothing in that slot); Weigh anchor stays the one control that ends the watch; the back arrow leaves the Pi watching, as today.
2. **Crew following another skipper's boat (`!ownPiWatch`): Leave stays**, unchanged — it is their only way to stop following (Weigh anchor is not theirs).
3. No behaviour change anywhere else: handleStopWatch, handleWeighAnchorFromShore, the session, the Pi keeper and alarms are untouched.
