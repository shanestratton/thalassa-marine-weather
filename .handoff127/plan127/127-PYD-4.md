## Decision (our recommendation)

**A. Places (places.ts)**
1. Skip atlas features with `likelyAnchorage === false` (not listed at all:
   they are not places to stop).
2. `PLACE_LIMIT` applies only to places that need a coastline ray-cast (OSM
   points without a baked table). Atlas places with baked tables are all ranked
   (63 at Airlie after the likelyAnchorage filter: proto-results.json
   `candidatesAll`; 74 is today's All places count including the nav names,
   no-anchoring and over-cap rows). Reviewed stops stay always ranked.
   Note (critic 2026-10-10): the cap saves no ray-casting today, because
   gatherPlaces ray-casts every OSM point inside the OSM loop
   (places.ts:501-523), before the reach and 1 NM filters; 10 commit 2 moves
   the ray-cast after them. Keep the cap here for planStop/CPU, not for the
   ray-cast.
3. `NEAR_CARD_NM = 3` (new, today.ts): the card never shows a stop under 3 NM
   or under 30 min at her cruising speed; All places still lists from
   `NEAR_START_NM` (1 NM).

**B. A free plan for every place (today.ts)**
4. `areaLegs(hours, model)`: StopLegs built from the point block already loaded
   (7 models): headline = the chart's model's series at the point, spread =
   the members, sea = null, flagged `area: true`. Zero requests.
5. `planStop` runs for every ranked place on `areaLegs` (the polar walk, so the
   light check uses her real passage time, not cruise speed: this retires the
   cruise-speed "home after dark" miss). With `area: true`, an unread sea does
   not cap the level (the route check reads it); the plan is labelled "on the
   area wind" wherever it shows. Memoised per (date, stay, place, limits,
   speed); target ≤ 150 ms for 74 places on the Mac in vitest (measured 35-58
   ms for 51).
6. Rank score, in order: level (Inside > Near > Unknown > Over) → stay grade →
   a composite (higher is better) → straight distance → id. Reviewed becomes a
   bonus, not a trump. **The composite has exactly four named terms and no
   time term (critic 2026-10-10):**
   - **stay** = the stay score, 0-100 (`areaStay`, today.ts:1014-1036);
   - **comfort** = `departureScore` of the place's best area-wind departure
     and NOTHING else (passageDepartureSuggestion.ts:95-101: `maxWindKts +
     0.35 x maxHeadwindKts + 0.25 x maxGustKts (if gusts) + 5 x maxWaveM (if
     waves)`, lower is calmer), turned into 0-100 against HER limits:
     `100 x clamp(1 - score / scoreAtLimits, 0, 1)`, where `scoreAtLimits` is
     the same formula with her poor wind, gust and wave limits and her poor
     wind as the headwind. The gust and wave terms are used only if EVERY
     ranked place has them (`departureScoreTerms` over all ranked places, not
     per place, :90-92), so places are compared on the same terms;
   - **day-out credit** = `min(estNm, 12) x 1.5`, minus 20 under 4 NM (the
     prototype's term, scratchpad/pyd127/proto.pyd.test.ts:125-127), scaled
     to this composite. It is named as its own term, not as comfort, because
     `departureScore` has no distance or time term: a passage-time penalty
     (the earlier draft's "time under way") made the nearest bombproof bays
     win best fit in every light wind (measured without the reviewed
     tie-break: Funnel 2.2 / Swamp 4.4 / Woodwark 7.5 NM, a new "same
     three"). The credit does the opposite, so a real day out beats a hop
     when both fit; SHORT HOP and CLOSER still give the hop a slot as an
     alternate;
   - **reviewed** = +10 when Local notes exist.
   Time under way appears only in words (the why line, 11) and in the SHORT
   HOP tag (10), never in the score.

**C. The pick (pure, deterministic: `pickStops(ranked, ctx)`)**
7. Best fit = rank 1 (excluding < NEAR_CARD_NM).
8. Second = the best-ranked place ≥ 45° of bearing from the best fit (from the
   start) and ≥ 3 NM from it; if none, the next by rank.
9. Third = "somewhere different": the best-ranked remaining place ≥ 3 NM from
   both, that earns a tag the second does not have, preferring NEW TO YOU, then
   SHORT HOP / CLOSER, then MORE SHELTER; if none, next by rank.
10. Tags are computed relative to the best fit (rules in "What the skipper
    sees"); the best fit itself has no tag (its badge comes in 7). NEW TO YOU
    reads voyage ends on the phone only; signed out or no history = no such tag.
    `getCachedSummaries` returns null when the cache is stale or unread
    (VoyageSummaryCache.ts:39-45): null means history UNKNOWN, so no NEW TO YOU
    at all (never "new to you" on every place). Count only real voyages
    (`isPlannedRoute` false; `isImported` counts) with a finite
    `lastLat`/`lastLon`. (Critic 2026-10-10.)
11. Why line: one sentence from the winning terms (shelter reason
    `stay.reasons[0]`, point of sail out/home from the plan's `how`, time under
    way, depth at the pin, history). Never a claim the plan does not support.

**D. Route-check five, show three**
("Route check" here = the route WEATHER legs, as today's `needsLegs`; it is
not autorouting. Say "weather along the way" in anything Shane reads: the
answer's "Five stops get a route check" can be read as five autoroutes.)
12. `ROUTE_CHECK_STOPS = 5`: the three picks plus the next two the pick rules
    would choose. `needsLegs` names five (6 → 10 proxy requests in parallel;
    the wait stays ~one request, 2-3 s measured).
13. Backfill: when a pick's route check is Over or failed and a backup is
    Inside/Near, the backup takes that slot and its tag is recomputed. While a
    stop's page is open, that stop is pinned to its slot (no swap under her);
    rows are found by id (`view.rows.get(id)`: 4 adds this map, merging before 5, which fills it for every place; one name
    for both packages).
    A routed plan from 2 counts as that stop's check: if the routed line turns
    a pick ✕ (e.g. home after dark on the longer line), it stays ✕ in its slot
    while its page is open and the backfill applies when the page closes. 2's
    rule "routes never feed preRank" stands; this is the pick, after ranking.
    (Critic 2026-10-10: otherwise 2's "card row shows the routed times" can
    leave a ✕ on the card while a checked backup fits.)
14. `SWEEP_STOPS` stays 3 for the card (2 below 640 px tall, unchanged).

**E. Depth at the pin (verdict only, memory only)**
15. For the five route-checked stops, `EncHazardService.queryHazards` at the
    pin (dynamic import; in-memory cells; no ETOPO, no network). Default boat
    or no cells → "depth not checked". Kept in the view only; never stored
    (licence rule 3).
    **What `queryHazards` really returns (critic 2026-10-10, re-verified):**
    - It folds every covering cell's `queryPoint` through the most-severe
      merge (services/enc/EncHazardService.ts:337-383).
    - `covered` means the pin is INSIDE a charted feature (a depth area,
      land, or a point/line hazard within the guard radius); a pin in a gap
      where the cell charts nothing is `covered: false` (EncSpatialIndex.ts:
      684-705). A lone spot sounding never grants coverage (`soundingOnly`,
      enc/types.ts:738-745).
    - Depth areas (DEPARE/DRGARE) are hazards ONLY below
      `ENC_HAZARD_DEPTH_M` = 15 m (EncSpatialIndex.ts:333-350,
      `classifyHazard`): a pin in an area charted 15 m or deeper comes back
      `covered: true, hazard: false, minDepthM: null`. A depth area with no
      DRVAL1 is treated as 'shallow' with `minDepthM: null`.
    - `minDepthM` of a depth area is its **DRVAL1, the shallow end of the
      area's depth range**, not a sounding at the pin: the water at the pin is
      somewhere from DRVAL1 to DRVAL2, and DRVAL2 is not returned. So words
      say "charted from N m", and a DRVAL1 under her need is "may need tide",
      never "needs tide".
    Every case:
    - not covered → "depth not checked" (no cap);
    - covered, no hazard → "15 m or more charted at the pin" (Inside allowed);
    - 'shallow', `minDepthM` ≥ draft + 0.5 → "charted from N m at the pin"
      (no cap);
    - 'shallow', 0 ≤ `minDepthM` < draft + 0.5 → level capped at Near, "may
      need tide at the anchorage (charted from N m)";
    - 'shallow', `minDepthM` < 0 → Near, "dries at the pin (charted)";
    - 'shallow', `minDepthM` null → "depth not charted at the pin" (no cap);
    - 'land' or 'coast' → "pin is on charted land: the stop is the water off
      it" (no cap; the router's pin rules handle the route end; common for a
      `natural=bay` way's centre);
    - 'rock' | 'wreck' | 'obstruction' → Near, "charted {rock} near the pin"
      (a point hazard within the guard radius, POINT_HAZARD_GUARD_RADIUS_M);
    - `soundingOnly` → "depth not checked" plus the sounding as a caution
      ("a charted sounding of N m near the pin"), never as coverage.

**F. Words**
16. Unreviewed places: "Mapped bay · not reviewed" in the stop page and All
